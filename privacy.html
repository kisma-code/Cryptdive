PRIVACY POLICY

Cryptdive · Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy tells you what data Cryptdive ("the game", "we") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The game is made by the developer of Cryptdive.

Short version: We save your Google login, your first name and your game progress. Other players see only your first name and your best floor. We do not sell your data.

1. Data we collect

  • Google login. When you sign in with Google, Google Firebase Authentication keeps your Google account ID, email address, display name and profile picture link. Only the game owner can see your email. We use it to keep your record safe and to answer you if you contact us.
  • Game record. We save your first name, best floor, best level, best kills, last class, number of runs, class Souls and class upgrades, and the time of the last save. This data is in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Data on your device. The game keeps your best floor, sound setting, last class and class progress in your browser storage, so the game works offline. Firebase also keeps your login on your device.
  • Technical data. The game website is hosted by GitHub Pages, and login and saves use Google Firebase. These services may receive your IP address and browser details when you load the game, as part of normal web traffic.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or payment data.

2. What other players see

The leaderboard shows your first name and your best floor to all players. It never shows your email.

3. Ads

The game may show ads in the future, from ad partners such as Google. Ad partners may use cookies or device identifiers to show ads and to measure them. You can read how Google uses data from sites that use its services, and you can change your ad settings at My Ad Center. When ads start, we will update this policy.

4. Who we share data with

We use these service providers to run the game: Google Firebase (login and saves) and GitHub Pages (website hosting). We do not sell your personal data. We share data only when the law requires it.

5. Children

The game is for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 gave us data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your record while you use the game. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

7. Your choices

  • Delete your data: email us from your Google email, and we will delete your login and your game record.
  • See your data: email us, and we will send you the data we keep about you.
  • Device data: you can clear it in your browser settings (website data).

8. Security

Your data is protected by Google Firebase security rules: each player can change only their own record. No system is 100% safe, but we try to keep your data safe.

9. Changes

We may change this policy. We will change the date at the top when we do.
